# Industrial Brutalist

- Composition: create a strong masthead, a headline/graphic split for expressive pages, and an indexed production list. Operational screens begin with their controls and data instead of a decorative hero.
- Hierarchy: bold display text at 3-4 times body size, section headings at 1.5 times, and monospaced identifiers at 0.8-0.875 times. Keep general Korean UI text on a readable sans stack.
- Structure: use the confirmed border width and radius consistently, a disciplined grid, and one primary accent. Use the padding token as a section unit; align indices and names across rows.
- Focal point: choose a strong typographic or geometric study tied to the subject. Contrast a large headline with compact metadata; keep body copy and control labels calm and readable.
- Controls: use clear pressed, focused and disabled states. Hard shadows or physical translation are optional and must respect confirmed shadow/radius tokens and reduced-motion preferences.
- Responsive: stack the split, reduce headline scale, wrap metadata under item names and retain usable 44px touch targets at 320px. Avoid forcing desktop grids to shrink below legible sizes.
- Preserve user tokens and references. Inspect actual desktop/mobile renders for alignment, contrast, text wrapping and interaction feedback before handoff.
