# Knowledge Workspace

- Identity: a quiet team knowledge workspace with true white document, off-white side rail and one understated blue accent. The Notion name is a style reference, not a copied identity.
- Composition: searchable document navigation, breadcrumb, shallow photographic cover, readable document title and metadata, a concise callout, table of contents and a working checklist. Do not turn the document into a marketing hero or metrics dashboard.
- Typography: constrain the document reading measure, use strong section hierarchy, and keep body copy and Korean fallback comfortable at normal sizes. Respect confirmed font and spacing tokens.
- Media: use a calm original editorial cover with no overlaid UI text; preserve its crop while allowing user-provided subject matter to replace the sample vase photograph.
- Interaction: document search has a clear empty state, selecting a document changes its real content and active navigation, and checkboxes update completion visibly. Use native controls and keyboard focus.
- Data: fictional FIELDNOTES documents demonstrate the design. They must not be inserted as the user's actual team policies or meeting records.
- Responsive: turn the sidebar into compact navigation, stack the table of contents below prose and keep checkboxes and related-document links reachable at 320px. Verify desktop and mobile rendering with the confirmed tokens.
