# Botanical Atelier

- Scope: borrow visual elements only. Features, routes, data and actions come from the user request and existing app, not this sample. Add sample functionality only when explicitly requested; preserve required functionality even when the sample lacks it.
- Identity: ivory paper, forest typography, olive accents, sunlight and a slower visual rhythm. Borrow organic shapes and materials without turning the requested app into a care brand.
- Composition: when imagery serves the task, offset a generous serif heading against a tall arched photograph. Use fine rules and quiet horizontal grouping for actual content; product rituals or shopping sections are not required.
- Typography: DM Serif Display or a locally available organic serif, generous title scale, short italic emphasis and readable body type with Korean fallback.
- Media: original or licensed subject-relevant photography with natural light and stone, linen or botanical texture where appropriate. Preserve the focal point inside the arched crop; sample products and claims are not app data.
- Detail: restrained olive actions, cream surfaces and thin green borders. Keep labels tied to the actual workflow; never invent medical claims or sustainability certifications.
- Responsive: stack related copy and image, preserve the arch where used and let real labels wrap at 320px. Keep whitespace without empty full-screen sections.
- Behaviour: retain required actions and their actual results. Motion is quiet and optional; verify loading, focus, contrast and mobile crop with confirmed tokens.
