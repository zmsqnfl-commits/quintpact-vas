# Botanical Atelier

- Identity: an organic care brand with ivory paper, forest typography and olive accents. The botanical subject, sunlight and slower rhythm are central.
- Composition: a generous serif headline beside a tall arched photograph, followed by three quiet product rituals separated by fine rules. Include one small botanical seal rather than decorating every surface.
- Typography: DM Serif Display or a locally available organic serif; generous title scale, short italic accent and readable simple body type with Korean fallback.
- Media: sunlit foliage, wildflowers, green glass and natural stone or linen. Crop with an arched top while preserving the product and enough plant context. Use original or licensed relevant photography.
- Detail: restrained olive action, cream surfaces and thin botanical-green borders. Avoid medical claims or invented sustainability certifications in sample copy.
- Responsive: stack copy then image; keep the arch tall and let ritual labels wrap naturally at 320px. Preserve whitespace without forcing empty full-screen sections.
- Behaviour: ritual actions show usable product or routine information. Keep motion quiet and optional; verify image loading, keyboard focus, text contrast and mobile crop against the selected tokens.
