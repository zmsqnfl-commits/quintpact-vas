# Noir Luxe

- Identity: a fragrance or luxury editorial with near-black, warm ivory and champagne gold. Luxury comes from proportion, photography and typographic restraint, not glossy badges everywhere.
- Composition: a fine-rule masthead, a large serif statement in the left half and a tall dramatic product photograph at right. A quiet three-note composition strip follows; no generic dashboard metrics.
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond at light weight or an elegant local serif for display and a clean small sans or mono for labels. Allow short italic emphasis and broad whitespace; Korean titles use a compatible serif fallback.
- Media: amber glass, dark stone, gold reflection and a directional spotlight. Keep the product sharply visible with intentional negative space and do not overlay paragraphs on busy highlights.
- Detail: square edges, sparse hairlines, modest warm-gold actions and widely spaced small labels. Use genuine product attributes rather than invented ratings or scarcity.
- Responsive: headline first, then a tall uncropped product image and readable scent notes. Preserve image focal point at 320px and desktop; avoid shrinking all text to fit the desktop layout.
- Behaviour: collection action opens a relevant product story. Motion is a restrained reveal only; reduced motion remains static. Confirm contrast on the actual image and on all interactive states.
