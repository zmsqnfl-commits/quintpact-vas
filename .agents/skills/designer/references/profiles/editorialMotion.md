# Editorial Motion

- Scope: borrow visual elements only. Features, routes, data and actions come from the user request and existing app, not this sample. Add sample functionality only when explicitly requested; preserve required functionality even when the sample lacks it.
- Composition: use a 7:5 heading/visual balance where expressive content supports it, with open aligned sections. Operational screens retain task-focused layouts; a masthead, portfolio index or editorial article is not required.
- Hierarchy: for display areas, a dominant heading at 3-4 times body size, secondary headings at 1.5 times and quiet metadata at 0.8 times. Scale down for functional screens. Use short Korean lines, word-break: keep-all, and 1.2 heading / 1.7 body leading.
- Rhythm: confirmed spacing is one unit, with 2 units between sections and half a unit within related content. Align text and imagery to one grid rather than framing every block.
- Focal point: one relevant photograph, illustration or typographic study when the task benefits from it. Preserve aspect ratios; no empty coloured rectangles standing in for required media.
- Details: fine section rules, small secondary labels and primary fill reserved for the main requested action. Do not fabricate project categories, popularity metrics or sample copy.
- Responsive: collapse expressive splits in reading order below 700px and move secondary labels below main content. Prevent long Korean headings from clipping.
- Motion: one short reveal or restrained hover is optional; respect reduced motion and keep required reading and navigation available immediately.
- Confirm tokens and references first. Verify hierarchy, crop where used, alignment, keyboard focus and 320/390px plus desktop layouts before handoff.
