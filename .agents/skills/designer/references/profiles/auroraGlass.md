# Aurora Glass

- Identity: a midnight workspace lit by cyan and violet aurora, with restrained translucent surfaces. Use depth and luminous material as the visual focus.
- Composition: a full-bleed aurora image, left-aligned large statement, an offset frosted workspace on the right and a compact centred dock below. Let light remain visible between surfaces; do not fill the page with opaque summary cards.
- Typography: Space Grotesk or a comparable geometric sans with Korean system fallback. Use a light-to-medium oversized title, small crisp labels and readable project names.
- Material: one atmospheric image behind glass, hairline translucent edges, inner highlight and backdrop blur. Provide an opaque dark fallback when blur is unavailable. Text contrast must be checked against the composed background, not only the token swatch.
- Media: keep the left side of the background dark and quiet for text; use cyan-violet light and reflected liquid on the right. Never flatten the entire interface into an image.
- Responsive: stack the statement above the workspace when its rows become cramped; retain readable glass padding and a small three-item dock. Test 320px and desktop.
- Behaviour: workspace actions reveal real content. Any ambient motion is slow, optional and paused for reduced motion. Confirm selected tokens while preserving text contrast and the light/glass separation.
