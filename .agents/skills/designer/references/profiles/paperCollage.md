# Paper Collage

- Identity: a personal editorial journal with blush paper, chocolate serif text and tangible analogue photographs. Preserve the warmth of collected objects.
- Composition: a quiet masthead, left-aligned editorial title and overlapping photo prints on the right, followed by two issue entries on fine rules. Avoid a repeated grid of identical cards.
- Typography: DM Serif Display or a compatible literary serif, generous readable body leading, small issue numbers and restrained handwritten accents only where legible.
- Media: actual wildflower, architecture and coastline photographs arranged as rotated ivory prints with paper tape and soft natural shadows. The collage may be a dedicated image asset; headings, links and article content remain native HTML.
- Detail: subtle paper grain, asymmetry contained within a clear reading grid and modest underlined or solid actions. Do not put large paragraphs over the photographs or flatten the whole page into a mockup image.
- Responsive: move the photo collage below the introduction, then stack issue entries with a horizontal separator. Preserve image proportions and avoid rotated-photo overflow at 320px.
- Behaviour: journal action opens a readable entry. Keep motion static or minimal, respect reduced motion and verify image loading, focus, contrast and the selected tokens on desktop and mobile.
